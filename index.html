    Sun: the MeteoSwiss ICON-CH1 and ICON-CH2 ensembles through Open-Meteo. Each of their 21 to 32 runs is a plausible forecast; the percentage is the share of runs that give you at least 20 minutes of sun (or half the time left, if less). The sun counts only when it is above the local terrain, from a 90 m elevation model. Best trade-off ranks places by expected sun minus the time spent driving there. Drives: OpenStreetMap routing, without traffic. Restaurants: OpenStreetMap.
